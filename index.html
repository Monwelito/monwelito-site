Case study 01 · Dental clinic · Philippines

Generating high-quality appointment leads for a local dental clinic

The problem

A newly opened dental clinic needed a reliable system for turning interest into booked appointments. As a new practice, the strategy called for a small, low-risk starting budget of ₱100/day over an initial 3-day test window to validate demand before committing more spend.

The solution

  • Set the campaign to Ad Set Budget Optimization (ABO) for direct control over daily spend across ad variations.
  • Started lean: ₱100/day over a 3-day test run to gauge performance with a cold, untested audience.
  • Targeted a cold audience in the clinic's service area to build awareness among people who hadn't heard of the practice yet.
  • Built a Meta Instant Form asking only for name, email, and phone, kept fast to reduce drop-off.
  • Ran simple image-based ad creatives for fast load times and direct messaging.

Case study 02 · Dental clinic · United States

Filling the chair with new patients for a family dental clinic

The problem

This clinic had empty spots in its schedule every week. They tried boosting posts, but they only got likes, not new patients. They wanted more people to book a cleaning or check-up.

What I did

  • Made a simple offer: a free check-up with every new patient cleaning.
  • Showed the ads only to people living close to the clinic, so it was easy for them to visit.
  • Used a short form. People only typed their name, phone, and email.
  • Called new leads back fast, the same day, so they did not forget.
  • Turned off the ads that did not work and gave more money to the best one.

Case study 03 · Insurance agent · United States

Getting more people to ask for life insurance quotes

The problem

An insurance agent was buying leads from other companies. The leads cost a lot, and many people did not even remember asking. The agent wanted fresh leads that came straight to them.

What I did

  • Wrote ads in easy words: “Protect your family. Get a free quote in 2 minutes.”
  • Showed the ads to parents and homeowners in the states where the agent can sell.
  • Asked 3 simple questions in the form, like age and how much cover they want, so the agent knew who was serious.
  • Sent each new lead to the agent right away by text and email.
  • Tested different pictures and kept the one that brought the cheapest leads.
